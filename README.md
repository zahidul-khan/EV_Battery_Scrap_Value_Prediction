# EV_Battey_Scrap_Value_Prediction
IONCOR Oy Scrap Value Forecasting Dashboard (Random Forest Regressor, XGBoost, ARIMA, LSTM, Prophet)
IONCOR operates in a business environment where the large industrial batteries used in its products degrade to varying degrees and retain residual value depending on their use, this research faces the problem of operational inefficiency. This is due to the lack of a systematic, evidence-based process for determining whether the components of end-of-life batteries can be reused directly or in secondary applications or require metallurgical recycling. The current lack of such a process result, in waste of materials and greatly increases its cost, which is estimated to be about 4 times higher than the cost of recycling. The study provides a methodological contribution by applying design science in a context of industrial recycling, an empirical contribution by being the first research to perform a comprehensive, to the smallest degree possible, assessment of various comparison models on EV batteries found in the literature, and finally a practical contribution in that IONCOR OY can now rely on a design plan that guides their process towards optimized performance.
